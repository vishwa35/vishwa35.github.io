      <ul>
      	<li>Cliffs along Ocean Path</li>
      	<ul>
		    <li>Trail or drive along the coast with parking lots and scenic stops</li>
		    <li>Highlights: Otter Cliffs and Thunder Hole</li>
		</ul>
		<li>Sand Beach</li>
      	<ul>
		    <li>Nice and cold water, north side of Ocean Path starts here</li>
		    <li>Note: Northmost part of the trail is less exciting</li>
		</ul>
		<li>Cadillac Mountain</li>
      	<ul>
		    <li>Highest peak, hike or drive up</li>
		    <li>Sunrise is beautiful, sometimes the first in the US to see it</li>
		    <li>*Permit required during summer to drive up*https://recreation.gov</li>
		</ul>
		<li>Jordan Pond</li>
      	<ul>
    		<li>Large pond surrounded by mountains</li>
    		<li>Teahouse/cafe open in summer, might need a reservation</li>
    		<li>Easy trail around the pond, connects to other trails</li>
		</ul>
		<li>Eagle Lake and Echo Lake</li>
      	<ul>
    		<li>Internal water viewpoints, less time spent here</li>
		</ul>
      </ul>
      </div>
      <div class="tab-pane fade" id="nav-2" role="tabpanel" aria-labelledby="nav-2-tab">
      <ul>
        <li>Bubbles Trail</li>
        <ul>
    		<li>Moderate to hard, rock scrambling</li>
    		<li>Balanced rock and summit of North Bubble</li>
    	</ul>
		<li>Jordan Pond Loop</li>
      	<ul>
    		<li>Easy, half boardwalk and half dirt path</li>
		</ul>
		<li>Beehive Trail</li>
        <ul>
    		<li>Steep ascent, iron rings to help climb</li>
    		<li>Great views of the Atlantic, comes down via the Bowl Trail</li>
    	</ul>
		<li>The Bowl Trail</li>
        <ul>
    		<li>More chill way to the top, pond at the top</li>
    	</ul>
		<li>Gorham Mountain Trail</li>
        <ul>
    		<li>Good views of Otter Cliffs and the ocean</li>
    	</ul>
		<li>Ocean Path Trail</li>
        <ul>
    		<li>Easy, walking along the coastline, peaceful and good views</li>
    	</ul>
		<li>Combined Trails (Including Above)</li>
        <ul>
	    	<li><a href="https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/maine/beehive-the-bowl-gorham-mountain-ocean-drive-loop">Beehive, The Bowl, Gorham Mountain, Ocean Drive Loop</a></li>
	    	<li><a href="https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/maine/bubbles-divide-via-jordans-pond-path">Bubbles Divide via Jordans Pond Path</a></li>
    	</ul>
      </ul></div>
      <div class="tab-pane fade" id="nav-3" role="tabpanel" aria-labelledby="nav-3-tab">
      <ul>
		<li>New Haven, CT</li>
    		<ul>
    			<li>Stopped for coffee, Yale University nearby</li>
    		</ul>
		<li>Mystic, CT</li>
    		<ul>
    			<li>Small town, easy to walk around, shops and ice cream</li>
    		</ul>
		<li>Providence, RI</li>
    		<ul>
    			<li>Nice downtown and waterfront, beautiful state library</li>
    			<li>Symposium books and boba, vegan donuts and food hall</li>
    		</ul>
		<li>Newport, RI</li>
    		<ul>
    			<li>Cliff Walk and mansions</li>
    		</ul>
		<li>Portsmouth, NH</li>
    		<ul>
    			<li>Fun to walk around, good food stop</li>
    			<li>Green Elephant vegetarian restaurant and smoothies at The Green Room</li>
    		</ul>
		<li>Portland, ME</li>
    		<ul>
    			<li>Eat at Green Elephant, walk around main area and old port</li>
    			<li>Lighthouses nearby</li>
    		</ul>
		<li>Freeport, ME</li>
    		<ul>
    			<li>Outlets for outdoor gear stores, nice place to stretch legs</li>
    		</ul>
		<li>Bangor, ME</li>
    		<ul>
    			<li>Surprisingly nice town, Chimera Coffee and main area walk</li>
    		</ul>
      </ul>
