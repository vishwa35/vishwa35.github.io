## Food

- Homeslice for pizza (try the pomegranate tahini cauliflower chickpea pizza)
- Inamo - Japanese food
- The Kati Roll Company
- Dishoom - overrated but worth trying once
- Nandos - gotta go once at least
- DUM Biryani House
- Crosstown Doughnuts
- Roti Chai - Indian street food
- Tibits and Soho vegan market (all veg/vegan menus, vegan options widely available)
- Hoppers - Amazing Sri Lankan food
- Ping Pong - Japanese
- Pachamama - Peruvian

### Outside of London

- **Oxford or Cambridge** - old University centered city feel
- **Bletchley Park** - if interested in Turing/WW2, cool museum

### Getting Around

- Get an Oyster Card for the tube (great for central London)
- Figure out frequency of use (weekly pass may not be worth it since the city is very walkable)
- Oyster Card has fare capping - you'll only be charged max amount per day/week
