London

Food#

  • Homeslice for pizza (try the pomegranate tahini cauliflower chickpea pizza)
  • Inamo - Japanese food
  • The Kati Roll Company
  • Dishoom - overrated but worth trying once
  • Nandos - gotta go once at least
  • DUM Biryani House
  • Crosstown Doughnuts
  • Roti Chai - Indian street food
  • Tibits and Soho vegan market (all veg/vegan menus, vegan options widely available)
  • Hoppers - Amazing Sri Lankan food
  • Ping Pong - Japanese
  • Pachamama - Peruvian

Places to Go#

  • Oxford Street - shopping hub
  • Picadilly Circus
  • Hyde Park
  • Regents Park
  • Tower Bridge
  • Thames River Cruise
  • Walk along the Thames
  • Anchor
  • Shakespeare’s Globe
  • Shard
  • London Eye
  • Borough Market - cool to walk around
  • Camden Market - lots of good food
  • Old Spitalfields - nice shopping and food
  • Brick Lane - Banglatown, less posh with great food (look for Chai Bros)
  • Imperial War Museum
  • Mr. Foggs Society - themed bars, really fun

Boroughs#

  • Marylebone / Mayfair
  • Kensington
  • Covent Garden
  • Soho
  • Westminster
  • Notting Hill / Kensington
  • Wembley (bit further out, India vibes)

Outside of London#

  • Oxford or Cambridge - old University centered city feel
  • Bletchley Park - if interested in Turing/WW2, cool museum

Getting Around#

  • Get an Oyster Card for the tube (great for central London)
  • Figure out frequency of use (weekly pass may not be worth it since the city is very walkable)
  • Oyster Card has fare capping - you’ll only be charged max amount per day/week
