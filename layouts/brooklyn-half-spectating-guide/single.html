📍 Best Spots to Cheer

Mile 1.5

Grand Army Plaza Loop

The race kicks off here! Tons of energy, good crowd. Early runners come through fast.

Mile 7

Fort Hamilton Parkway Station

Great mid-race spot. Runners are settled in, good steady pace. Easy parking in the area.

Miles 7–12

Ocean Parkway (All along here!)

This is where I recommend you go. Long stretch, less crowded than start/finish, great views. Runners are powered but not exhausted yet.

Mile 13.1

Coney Island Boardwalk (Finish)

The payoff moment! Super crowded but incredibly emotional. Come if you want the celebration energy.

✅ Day-Of Checklist

What should I bring?

▼
Water, snacks, phone charger, comfortable shoes, weather-appropriate layers, and a fun sign or noise maker!

How early should I arrive?

▼
For Ocean Parkway: 30 mins before my expected time. For the finish: 1-2 hours early for a good spot.

How will I know when I'm coming?

▼
Use the tracker link above with my bib number. Or text me and I'll let you know my pace!

Worried about crowds?

▼
Stick to Ocean Parkway (miles 7-12). Still plenty of energy but way less chaotic than start/finish lines.

Is parking terrible?

▼
Yes. Use the subway! Get off at the stop nearest your spectating spot and walk from there.
Questions? Text me! I'm just as nervous as you are 😄
